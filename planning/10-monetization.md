# 10 - Rekordly V2 Monetization

## Summary

Rekordly V2 should make money aggressively but without damaging financial trust or daily usability.

The Free plan should be useful enough to build a habit, but limited enough that serious users naturally upgrade. Paid plans should remove ads, increase limits, unlock professional outputs, and add high-value business features.

Monetization must never:

- Block critical financial actions with forced ads.
- Sell or expose user financial data.
- Interrupt transaction saving, invoice sending, wallet payments, loan repayments, or correction flows.
- Make the product feel unsafe for recording money.

## Revenue Streams

Primary revenue streams:

- Monthly subscriptions.
- Annual subscriptions with a discount.
- AI credit packs.
- Premium reports and exports.
- Assisted onboarding and data cleanup.
- Paid migration support from v1, CSV files, or spreadsheets.

Future revenue streams:

- WhatsApp Business API add-on.
- Team seats.
- Partner or affiliate offers for POS providers, banks, insurance, bookkeeping, tax filing, and business services.
- Optional payment-link platform fee after legal and business validation.

## Subscription Plans

The initial plan names are:

- Free
- Starter
- Business
- Pro

Paid users should never see ads.

### Free

Positioning: useful personal/business finance starter plan with ads and strict limits.

Includes:

- Ads enabled.
- 100 transactions per month.
- Limited AI Entry credits.
- Read-only offline access.
- Branded invoices with "Generated by Rekordly" footer or watermark.
- Single default workspace currency.
- Limited invoices.
- Limited exports.
- Basic budgeting.
- Limited simple loans and customer wallets.
- No Tax Readiness Reports by default.

Optional growth experiment:

- Allow 1 annual Tax Readiness Summary for free users to demonstrate value.

## Ads Strategy

Ads apply only to the Free plan.

### Display / Image Ads

Recommended placements:

- Dashboard lower section or side rail.
- Transaction list after several rows, away from primary action buttons.
- Reports or budget summary lower section.
- Settings or free-plan upgrade area.

Recommended frequency:

- Maximum 2 ad slots per desktop page.
- Maximum 1 ad slot per mobile screen.
- Maximum 15 display impressions per active user per day.
- No auto-refreshing ad slots unless explicitly allowed by the ad provider.

Display ads must not appear in:

- Login/signup/OTP screens.
- Transaction save flows.
- AI confirmation forms.
- Invoice send/download confirmation.
- Wallet payment flows.
- Loan repayment flows.
- Void/correction flows.
- Payment confirmation screens.

### Rewarded Ads

Rewarded ads must not be per-action gates.

Explicitly banned:

- Forcing a user to watch an ad before saving a transaction.
- Forcing a user to watch an ad before sending an invoice.
- Forcing a user to watch an ad before recording a payment.
- Forcing a user to watch an ad before correcting or voiding a financial record.

Allowed use:

- Rewarded ads can only expand a monthly usage pool after a user reaches a Free plan limit.

Example:

- User reaches 100 transactions for the month.
- App offers: "Watch 1 ad to unlock 10 more transactions this month."
- If completed and verified, backend grants 10 more transaction slots for that month.

Recommended rewarded ad caps:

- Maximum 3 rewarded videos per user per day.
- Maximum 20 rewarded videos per user per month.
- Maximum 1 grant per limit-expansion action.

Reward rules:

- Reward grants must be server-side.
- Reward grants must be idempotent.
- Reward should be granted only after verified completion where the ad provider supports completion callbacks or server verification.
- If an ad fails to load, show normal upgrade or wait options.

### Interstitial Ads

Interstitial or forced ads are excluded from MVP.

They may be reconsidered only if:

- User trust metrics remain healthy.
- Display and rewarded ads are not sufficient.
- Ads can be placed away from financial confirmation flows.

## Offline Monetization Rules

Offline support has two tiers.

### Free Users

Free users get read-only offline access.

They can view:

- Cached dashboard summaries.
- Cached transaction history.
- Cached invoice lists where available.

They cannot:

- Create offline transactions.
- Create offline invoices.
- Queue offline payments.
- Sync offline mutations.

If a Free user is offline and tries to create a record, show:

> Offline recording is a Premium feature.

### Paid Users

Paid users get:

- Offline draft creation.
- Offline mutation queue.
- Background sync when online.
- Server-wins conflict rules for finalized financial records.

Queue depth may still vary by plan.

## Premium Feature Gates

### Invoice Branding

- Free invoices and PDFs include "Generated by Rekordly."
- Paid plans remove Rekordly branding for a professional white-label look.

### Multi-Currency

- Free and Starter are locked to the workspace default currency.
- Business and Pro unlock multi-currency records and balances.
- MVP data model still stores currency codes for all plans.

### AI Entry Vs AI Assistant

- Free and Starter get AI Entry: text-to-draft transaction creation.
- Business and Pro get AI Assistant: asking questions about their financial data.
- AI Assistant must respect tenant isolation, privacy, rate limits, and plan limits.

### Tax Readiness Reports

- Raw Tax Readiness Reports are gated by plan limits.
- Free has no access by default, with 1 annual summary kept as an optional growth experiment.
- Starter gets quarterly Tax Readiness Reports.
- Business and Pro get on-demand or unlimited Tax Readiness Reports.
- If the user asks AI to "explain my tax summary," that explanation costs 1 AI credit.
- Raw tax report generation does not consume AI credits unless AI explanation is requested.

### WhatsApp Business API

- WhatsApp Business API integration is Phase 2.
- WhatsApp is strictly paid because it has per-conversation/provider costs.
- Business and Pro are eligible.
- Starter and Free are not eligible by default.

## Product And API Implications

Backend must enforce:

- Plan limits.
- Ad reward grants.
- Offline creation access.
- Invoice branding.
- Multi-currency access.
- Tax report access.
- AI Assistant access.
- WhatsApp eligibility.
- AI credit usage.

Later API/data model planning should include:

- `plans`
- `subscriptions`
- `usage_counters`
- `feature_limits`
- `ad_events`
- `rewarded_ad_grants`

Rules:

- Reward grants must expand monthly pools only.
- Reward grants must not unlock critical per-action financial steps.
- Reward grants must be idempotent.
- Ad blockers must not break core Free plan finance flows.
- Frontend may show ads, upgrade prompts, and locked UI.
- Backend remains the source of truth.
- Paystack is the first subscription provider.
- Stripe remains optional later.
- Webhooks must update subscription state idempotently.

## Source Notes

Pricing and ad policies change often. Re-check all sources before launch.

Checked on 2026-06-04:

- [Google AdSense ad placement policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1346295?hl=en): avoid accidental clicks, deceptive layouts, and misleading ad placement.
- [Google AdSense program policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en): do not inflate clicks or impressions and do not compensate users for normal non-rewarded ads.
- [Google AdMob rewarded ads](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7311747?hl=en): rewarded ads are designed for optional in-app rewards after users complete rewarded inventory.
- [Paystack pricing](https://paystack.com/pricing): use current Paystack fees when finalizing subscription and payment monetization.

## Test / Review Criteria

- Free users see ads only in approved low-risk placements.
- Paid users never see ads.
- No rewarded ad is required before saving or confirming any financial action.
- Rewarded ads only expand monthly usage pools.
- Reward grants are idempotent and server-side.
- Free users can view cached offline data but cannot create offline records.
- Paid users can use full offline draft and sync flows.
- Free invoices include Rekordly branding.
- Paid invoices remove Rekordly branding.
- Free and Starter remain single currency.
- Business and Pro unlock multi-currency.
- Tax reports are gated by plan.
- AI explanation of a tax summary consumes 1 AI credit.
- AI Assistant is Business and Pro only.
- WhatsApp API is Business and Pro eligible only.
- Plan limits are enforced server-side.
- Ad blockers do not break the app's core finance workflows.
- Monetization does not sell or expose user financial data.

## Assumptions

- MVP uses Paystack for paid subscriptions.
- Ads are Free-plan only.
- Display/image ads are MVP-safe in low-risk placements.
- Rewarded ads are optional monthly-limit expansion only.
- Interstitial and forced ads are excluded from MVP.
- WhatsApp remains Phase 2 and paid only.
- Raw Tax Readiness Reports are informational and not government filing.
- Final prices are placeholders and should be tested against real willingness to pay.
